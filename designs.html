Catapult

DESIGN 02

Mouse

In high school, my club, the Fabrication Lab, ran a charity toy sale during the annual Christmas show as a community project. We designed and 3D-printed the toys at school, sold them, and raised about $800, which we donated to charity. This is one of the toys I designed: a mouse with a body made of chain joints.
